Substance Use Therapy

Substance Use Therapy for Men in Denver, From Someone Who’s Done This Work

Individual and Group Therapy for Men Questioning Their Drinking or Drug Use

This Isn’t About Whether You’re an Addict

Most guys who end up in this conversation aren’t in crisis. They’re just noticing that a drink, or something stronger, has quietly become the thing that handles a hard day instead of dealing with it.

My Background Here Is Different

Before private practice, I worked in addiction treatment directly. This isn’t a modality I read about. It’s where I started.

Where We Start

We start with what the substance is doing for you, not what it’s doing to you. That’s the honest question, and it’s usually the one nobody’s asked you yet. From there, we look at the gap between what you say you want and what you’re currently doing, without turning it into a lecture.

Individual or Group

Some men need one-on-one space to say the specific thing they haven’t said out loud yet. Others need a room of men who aren’t going to flinch, dealing with the exact same thing. Both are on the table here, often both at once.
