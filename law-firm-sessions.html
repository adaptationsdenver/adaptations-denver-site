Fair questions

For the skeptical partner

Is this therapy?

No. It's education: how burnout, compassion fatigue and perfectionism work, how to spot them, and what helps. Nobody is diagnosed, assessed or treated.

Will anyone have to share something personal?

No. There's no going around the room, no partner exercises and no one put on the spot. People listen, and they can ask questions if they want to.

Our attorneys won't want to look weak in front of the partners.

That's exactly why the sessions are built the way they are. The material is about how these patterns show up in high performers in general, not about anyone in the room. Sitting through a talk on burnout says about as much about you as sitting through one on cybersecurity.

And the follow-up survey is anonymous.

What about confidentiality?

I don't take notes on attendees and I don't report back to the firm on individuals. The survey is anonymous. If your firm has its own requirements, tell me on the call and we'll work within them.

How long is a session?

Sixty minutes, including Q&A. That fits a lunch hour for most firms. If you need something shorter or longer, we'll set it on the call.

Can it be tailored to our firm?

Yes. Practice area, seniority, what's been happening at the firm: all of it shapes the examples and emphasis.

Can it be virtual?

Yes. In person in the Denver area, fully virtual, or hybrid. If some of your people are in the office and some are remote, everyone can join the same session.

What does it cost?

It depends on format and length. After the call you'll get a straightforward quote.
